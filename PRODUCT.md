## Platform

web

## Users

Used-car buyers in one market (Georgia today), two groups with equal weight:
- Private buyers who want a car for themselves and fear overpaying or buying a hidden wreck.
- Resellers and small dealers who buy below market to resell and care about speed.

They reach the site from social bio links (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), Telegram link previews and posts in car groups. Most are on phones. Many read Russian, Georgian or Persian before English.

## Product Purpose

CarHunter is a Telegram bot. It checks used-car listings from public car sites all day, compares each price with the market price of similar cars, and sends the cheap ones to the user first, by personal filters. The site is the door to the bot: success is a visitor who opens the bot (`t.me/CarHunterGE_bot?start=src_site`) and saves a filter. The site also hosts the legal pages (privacy, terms, data deletion) that Meta and TikTok require, and is the home of the CarHunter World brand, which will hold one bot per country.

## Positioning

The market price on every deal is a real number: the median of the same model and year (and engine, when there are enough cars), with the car count shown. The user sees the math, not a vague "good deal" badge. Deals arrive in a private Telegram chat, before they reach any public channel. A VIN check shows US auction history (Copart/IAAI: salvage title, damage), which matters because many cars here come from US auctions.

## Operating Context

- Everything happens in Telegram: a filter wizard (budget, discount, then optional make, age, mileage, customs, engine), deal cards in DM, a VIN check by text or photo, a garage of saved cars, a VIP channel.
- Deal card fields: discount (`WOW -27%`), make model year, engine, mileage, price (lari and USD), market price with sample size, city, customs status, link to the listing, buttons (Spot on / Not for me / Save).
- Free demo: 7 days, 5 cars a day, 2 VIN checks for the whole demo. The clock starts with the first car that arrives (or a VIN check, or after 3 days with no car), no card. The site states these numbers.
- Plans (shown in the bot): 7-day pass €5 (one payment, no auto-renewal, 7 VIN checks), VIP €14 / month (30 VIN checks, plain price, no promo since 2026-10-30), PRO €49 / month (100 VIN checks).
- Payment goes through Tribute inside Telegram. The bot never sees the card; the charge is made by TRBT Limited (Cyprus, HE 443898), which is what the bank statement shows. Fear of paying a new bot is real and must be answered openly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site generated by `_src/build.py` (Python, one template, texts per language in the script). Languages: en (`/`), ru (`/ru/`), ka (`/ka/`, Georgian script), fa (`/fa/`, Persian, right to left). Legal pages are English only.
- No JS framework and no Node build. Small inline JS is fine. Fonts load from Google Fonts for now (Fira Sans and Fira Sans Extra Condensed, Permanent Marker for prices; Noto Sans Georgian for ka, Vazirmatn for fa); self-hosting comes with the move to Azure Static Web Apps.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages now, moving to Azure Static Web Apps (Free) with a `/go/` click route.
- English copy is plain A2 level, short sentences. No em dash in any copy. Georgian and Persian texts are not yet checked by native speakers.
- Planned: a strip of fresh real deals, updated automatically from the content pipeline. Until it exists, deals on the page are real ones copied by hand with their date.

## Brand Commitments

- Names: CarHunter World (umbrella brand, domain carhunterworld.com), CarHunterGE (the bot).
- Logo: `logo.png`, a car on a navy gradient with a gold crosshair. Keep it.
- Social videos use a green accent (#27c97a) and a DIN-style face. These are existing assets, not binding for the site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real deal cards from the bot. Every number comes from the listing feed and the market baseline; nothing is rounded up or invented.
- Real bot texts and buttons (filter wizard, deal card, VIN check, plans, "How the payment works").
- Absent and never to be faked: testimonials, customer names or logos, user counts, ratings, press, savings totals.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with real deals, not claims.
2. Show the math: market price and how many cars it came from.
3. The site is a door to Telegram; one clear action.
4. Plain words in every language; the same truth in all four.
5. Answer the money fear before it is asked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark. Full right-to-left layout for Persian. Georgian has no capital letters: no uppercase transforms or synthetic italics on Georgian text.
