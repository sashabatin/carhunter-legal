How the payment works

The bot never sees your card.

The pay button opens Tribute's payment page. You enter the card there, and it never reaches us.

We store nothing about payment.

We keep your Telegram id, your plan, its end date and your filters. No card number to leak.

Who takes the money.

TRBT Limited (Cyprus, HE 443898), the company behind Tribute. This name shows on your bank statement.

No card? Fine.

Besides a card, Tribute offers other ways to pay. The set depends on your device and country, and you see the full list before you pay.

There is a service fee.

Tribute adds its own fee on top of the plan price. You see the full sum at checkout, before you confirm.

Cancel in one place.

You manage and cancel the plan inside Tribute.
