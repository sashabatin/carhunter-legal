CarHunter World. Last updated: 11 August 2026.

What the service does

CarHunter World is a Telegram bot. It watches public car listings, compares each price with the market median for the same segment, and sends you the ones that look underpriced. It is an information tool.

What it is not

  • We do not sell cars and we are not a dealer or a broker.
  • We are not a party to any deal you make with a seller.
  • A price estimate is a statistical guess, not a valuation. Always inspect the car yourself.
  • A vehicle history check shows what the data providers have. An empty result does not prove that a car is clean.

Listings and prices

Listing data comes from public sources and can be wrong, old, or already sold. We show it as we find it and we cannot guarantee that it is correct.
